## Editorial method

Narrated passages contain the factual narrative and reflective observation. Research limitations remain in the Sources panel and complete log-book transcript. Comparative domestic history identifies the other property explicitly. Physical features from marketing material are not presented as independently measured or confirmed original. No patient history, resident quotation, original artifact provenance, or specific maternity-room use is invented. Current plans remain conditional and dated.
